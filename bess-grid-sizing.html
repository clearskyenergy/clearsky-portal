Size Sweep

Method. Grid headroom is screened against feeder thermal capacity, utility planning margin, service conductor/breaker rating, and any stated interconnection or export cap. Peak shaving is dispatched interval-by-interval against a per-month target found by bisection, with charging constrained so the battery never sets a new peak. Residual warranty cycles are credited to TOU arbitrage at the on-peak/off-peak spread net of round-trip losses. Later-year revenue is scaled linearly by retained capacity rather than re-dispatched each year. This is a screening model — not a substitute for a utility interconnection study or an ISO capacity qualification review.
